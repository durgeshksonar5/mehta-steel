Our Iron & Steel Products

Quality Certified Stockists Since 1982

Comprehensive Iron & Steel
Product Portfolio

We supply a comprehensive inventory of certified iron and steel products sourced directly from India's premier primary producers (SAIL, Tata Steel, Jindal, Vizag Steel, Essar). From heavy structural members to precision sheets and tubes, we deliver quality materials with mill test certificates across India.

Structural Steel

High-tensile MS Angles, Equal & Unequal Channels, and MS Flats engineered for heavy construction, pre-engineered buildings, and industrial fabrication.

Industrial Plates

Prime mild steel plates, high-tensile chequered plates, and certified boiler quality (BQ) steel plates conforming to IS 2062 and ASTM specifications.

Industrial Sheets

Hot Rolled (HR) sheets, Cold Rolled Closed Annealed (CRCA) sheets, Galvanized Plain (GP), and Galvanized Corrugated (GC) roofing sheets in various gauges.

Industrial Beams

Heavy-duty ISMB, NPB, and WPB structural steel beams, joists, and columns designed for multi-storey frames, crane gantries, and industrial infrastructure.

Iron Bars & TMT

Fe 500D / 550D TMT reinforcement steel bars, MS Round Bars, Bright Bars, and Square Bars suited for civil construction and precision engineering components.

Pipes & Fittings

ISI-marked Mild Steel (MS) black pipes, Galvanized Iron (GI) pipes, seamless tubes, and industrial pipe fittings sourced from leading mills like Zenith & Apollo.
