About Our Process

We source certified iron and steel items directly from reputed primary mills such as Tata Steel, SAIL, Jindal, and RINL, ensuring verified chemistry and mechanical strength.

Our strategically located godowns in Kalamboli (Mumbai) and Khed Shivapur (Pune) feature segregated storage, ensuring swift order handling and protected material integrity.

Our quality auditors inspect every consignment for dimensional accuracy, standard weights, and durability, supported by manufacturer test certification.

With an established logistics network and dedicated transport coordination, we guarantee timely delivery of wholesale steel orders to your project site or factory.
