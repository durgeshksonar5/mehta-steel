About Mehta Steel Syndicate

View Products

Established in 1982 under the esteemed mentorship of Mr. Deepak Mehta, Mehta Steel Syndicate has evolved into a leading iron and steel trading, stockholding, and distribution firm serving industrial clients across India.

We are recognized as dependable iron and steel merchants, stockists, wholesalers, distributors, and suppliers. Operating from our registered office in Mumbai and branch office in Somwar Peth, Pune, we maintain massive inventory hubs in Khed Shivapur and Kalamboli to deliver prime certified steel products on time.

Wholesale Trading & Distribution

Comprehensive stocking of structural steel, plates, sheets, beams, bars, and pipes at competitive wholesale market rates.

Reliable Primary Vendor Network

Authorized associations and direct sourcing with SAIL, Tata Steel, Jindal, Vizag Steel (RINL), and Essar.

Client-Centric Solutions

Custom cutting, slitting, flexible scheduling, and dedicated sales support ensuring zero client downtime.
